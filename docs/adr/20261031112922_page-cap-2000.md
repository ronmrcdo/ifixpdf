# Cap a workspace at 2,000 pages

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-31

## Context

Audit finding #10. Nothing limited how many pages a workspace could hold, and the page grid
renders a card per page, so a very large PDF or a long Merge list could freeze the window.

## Decision

- A workspace holds at most `MAX_PAGES` (2,000) pages, in `src/state/workspace.ts`.
- `addFiles` and `replaceWith` refuse over the cap before `own()`, so nothing is retained for a
  refused file. `edit` refuses a change that is over the cap and also grows the page count, so
  duplicating past the cap does nothing, while removing pages from an over-cap workspace works.
- A refusal shows the toast "iFixPDF works with up to 2,000 pages at a time."
- A cap was chosen over a virtualized grid. Revisit the virtualized grid if users hit the cap.

## Consequences

- A PDF over 2,000 pages cannot be opened.
- Drafts restored by `restore` are not checked; they were saved under the same cap.
